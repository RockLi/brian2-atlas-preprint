## Gaps found and repaired

- NMDA: 112 first-party driver, analysis and source-description files were outside both repositories.
- Capacity: 66 experiment programs/protocols and exact frozen model generators were only in evidence directories or archives.
- Macaque MAM: the 249 research files were already imported, but three small protocol/activity fixtures were absent. Restoring them repaired the observed test failures; the expanded analysis selection passes 30 tests.
- External training qualification: 1,181 captured files now include adapters, protocols, small fixtures, and a sealed 984-file source snapshot/revision archive.
- Ninety-five explicitly referenced source contents were recovered from Git, local archives, remote generated-source directories or exact deterministic asset reconstruction. See [source versions](source-versions/README.md).

## Remaining boundaries

Three files from the old Figure 7 Neural Lab build remain unresolved: `index.html`, `lab.css`, and `pkg/b2_runner.js`. Current Atlas browser source and the original screenshot/experiment export are available, but they must not be relabeled as the exact old build. The online Figure S1 capture is an interface illustration with its original documented source-identity limit.

Pinned upstream model sources are obtained using their acquisition instructions. Large data and complete raw outputs remain outside Git. These are separate from the code gaps repaired above. Earlier intermediate FlyWire MNIST source versions retain the previously documented limitation; that development study is not a new reported numerical result in the final manuscript.

Later MAM seed1752 and closed-loop diagnostics, male-CNS/MNIST and affective work were inventoried but are outside the final paper's reported cohorts. They remain in their original branches/worktrees and are not silently ported across the approved product cutoff.
